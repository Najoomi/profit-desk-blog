Profit Desk publishes practical pricing and fee guides for small online sellers: how platform fees work, how to calculate your real profit per sale, and how to set a price that hits your target margin.

Fee figures are checked against each platform's official help pages and dated in every post. Fees change; verify current fees with each platform. Educational content, not financial or tax advice.
